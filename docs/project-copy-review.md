## Updated introductions

### NITECORE HC65 UHE

**Rechargeable outdoor headlamp**

The NITECORE HC65 UHE combines a primary beam, close-range white light and red light in a compact aluminum headlamp. Two differently shaped buttons make the main controls distinguishable by touch, while the removable bracket positions the lamp close to its center of gravity. The design balances lighting flexibility, direct operation and wearing comfort for outdoor use.

### COLBOR CL60

**Modular COB video light**

The COLBOR CL60 is a compact video light built around a modular body. Sliding connections allow individual fixtures to form a larger lighting array, making expansion part of the enclosure itself. A defined front light source, ventilated housing and adjustable support separate illumination, cooling and positioning into readable elements for studio and location setups.

### SYNCO P1S

**Wireless microphone system for smartphones**

The SYNCO P1S pairs a compact clip-on transmitter with a receiver that connects directly to a smartphone. A sliding charging case keeps the components together, bringing storage, charging and setup into one compact object. Slim forms and visible status lighting give the system a consistent identity while keeping the recording interface simple and easy to carry.

### SYNCO MC2

**Compact dual-channel audio mixer**

The SYNCO MC2 design brings a two-channel mixing interface into a compact enclosure. Paired gain and reverb knobs sit beneath a shared level display, giving the left and right channels a clear visual order. Connections are arranged on the sides, leaving the front face dedicated to adjustment and feedback. Rounded edges soften the otherwise precise, equipment-like form.

### COLBOR PL8-R

**Pocket RGB video light**

The COLBOR PL8-R brings adjustable color lighting into a slim, portable panel. Its broad luminous face is paired with a rear display and physical controls, separating light output from the operating interface. An integrated battery and mounting points support compact camera setups, while the flat enclosure makes the light easy to carry between shooting locations.

### SYNCO S6E

**Wired omnidirectional lavalier microphone**

The SYNCO S6E is a wired lavalier microphone for recording speech across cameras, phones and other compatible devices. A small inline module combines device recognition with a visible status indicator, reducing the need for manual mode changes. The clip-on microphone and six-metre cable keep the recording unit away from the speaker while allowing freedom of movement.

### SYNCO T3

**Dual-channel UHF wireless microphone system**

The SYNCO T3 is a wireless microphone system for recording two speakers through one receiver. Compact aluminum housings combine LCD status displays with dedicated physical controls, keeping channel selection and audio monitoring visible during a shoot. Belt clips and mounting points allow the same coordinated set of devices to move between body-worn and camera-mounted use.

### SYNCO TS

**Compact UHF wireless microphone system**

The SYNCO TS brings two transmitters and a receiver into a compact wireless recording system for interviews and filmmaking. Foldable antennas reduce the space needed for transport while allowing adjustment during use. A shared arrangement of displays, controls and connections gives the three units a consistent identity and keeps channel information readable during setup.

### SYNCO D2

**Hypercardioid XLR shotgun microphone**

The SYNCO D2 is a shotgun microphone for boom and camera recording. Its slender brass housing pairs a long acoustic tube with an XLR connection, giving the product a clear linear form. A dark, low-reflection finish keeps it visually unobtrusive on set, while the restrained exterior places the emphasis on positioning and sound capture.
