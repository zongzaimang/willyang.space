The SYNCO D2 translates a professional hypercardioid shotgun microphone into a restrained, production-ready form. Its long brass body gives the product visual stability and acoustic solidity, while a continuous pattern of diagonal openings turns a functional interference tube into the main design signature.

## A precise acoustic silhouette

The cylindrical architecture is kept deliberately simple so the microphone reads as one coherent instrument. Fine grooves, controlled transitions and minimal graphics preserve the visual length of the body, while the dark metallic finish reduces distraction when the product is used around cameras, booms and studio equipment.

![Close view of the D2 cylindrical body and interference-tube pattern](/assets/projects/190711-synco-d2/imports/20260921-local/02.jpg)

## Material and manufacturing

The brass construction informed both the proportion and surface treatment. The housing is designed as a robust linear shell, with machined details concentrated around the acoustic openings, connector and assembly boundaries. A low-reflectance black finish unifies the parts and supports professional on-set use.

## A complete product experience

The same disciplined visual language extends to presentation and packaging. Product, accessories and protective storage are organized as one system, giving the microphone a consistent professional identity from transport to recording.
