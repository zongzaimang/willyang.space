The SYNCO S6E is a compact wired lavalier microphone built around simple cross-device recording. Its inline control module consolidates automatic device recognition, battery access and status feedback in a small form that remains easy to understand along a six-metre cable.

## A compact inline architecture

The control module uses a soft cylindrical body to align naturally with the cable and avoid unnecessary visual bulk. A clear split between upper and lower sections communicates the battery-access point, while generous radii make the object comfortable to handle and less likely to catch during setup.

![SYNCO S6E lavalier microphone and six-metre cable](/assets/projects/200520-synco-s6e/imports/20260921-local/01.jpg)

## Quiet visual feedback

A single front LED communicates operating status without adding a complex interface. The restrained black CMF allows the microphone, clip and cable to remain discreet on clothing, while the geometric band around the lower body gives the compact controller a recognizable product identity.

![S6E microphone, clip, connector and LED indicator](/assets/projects/200520-synco-s6e/imports/20260921-local/02.jpg)

## Battery access without extra controls

The centre section rotates to open the battery compartment, integrating maintenance into the main body instead of adding a separate door. This keeps the silhouette clean and makes the interaction discoverable through the visible material break and textured graphic band.

![S6E rotating centre section for battery installation](/assets/projects/200520-synco-s6e/imports/20260921-local/03.jpg)
