The SYNCO D30 is a camera-mounted shotgun microphone designed around direct, visible control. Its slim acoustic barrel is paired with a compact control body, making gain, low-cut filtering and status feedback accessible without interrupting the recording setup.

## Function expressed through form

The front section uses a repeated curved slot pattern to visually communicate sound capture while protecting the microphone structure. Behind it, the control body becomes denser and more tactile: distinct buttons, indicator lights, USB-C charging and a knurled gain dial are arranged as a clear operating sequence.

## Precision in use

The stepped control dial provides an immediate physical reference for gain adjustment, while separated filter controls reduce ambiguity during fast camera work. Blue and green light accents are used sparingly against the matte-black body so operational feedback remains visible without dominating the product.

![D30 side profile with updated identity graphics](/assets/projects/190826-synco-d30/imports/20260921-local/03.png)

![D30 microphone and integrated adjustment dial](/assets/projects/190826-synco-d30/imports/20260921-local/04.png)

## Isolating handling noise

The microphone and camera are connected through a dual-ring shock mount. Its open geometry keeps the assembly visually light, protects access to the controls and creates a stable transition from the long barrel to the compact camera interface.
