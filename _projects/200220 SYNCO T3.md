## Dual-channel UHF wireless microphone system

The SYNCO T3 is a wireless microphone system for recording two speakers through one receiver. Compact aluminum housings combine LCD status displays with dedicated physical controls, keeping channel selection and audio monitoring visible during a shoot. Belt clips and mounting points allow the same coordinated set of devices to move between body-worn and camera-mounted use.
