The SYNCO T3 is a two-person UHF wireless microphone system developed for camera, mobile and field-production workflows. Two transmitters and a dual-channel receiver share a rugged compact enclosure, allowing the system to feel coherent while supporting different input, output and monitoring tasks.

## A field-ready product family

Each unit uses a strong perimeter frame, protected corners and a recessed central interface. The repeated layout establishes a clear family identity across receiver and transmitters, while dedicated labels, ports and switches help users distinguish functions at a glance.

![SYNCO T3 receiver and transmitters in a dark studio composition](/assets/projects/200220-synco-t3/imports/20260921-local/01.jpg)

![SYNCO T3 units shown from multiple working angles](/assets/projects/200220-synco-t3/imports/20260921-local/02.jpg)

## Controls organized for recording

The screen and primary buttons are positioned on the front face for direct access, with audio connections and channel controls distributed along the edges. Twin antennas on the receiver express its dual-channel capability and create a balanced silhouette when the system is mounted on a camera.

## Robust detail language

Long recessed ribs add grip, protect the broad surfaces and visually connect the display area with the lower housing. Matte black finishes keep the system discreet around production equipment, while small gloss and graphic accents clarify the active controls.
