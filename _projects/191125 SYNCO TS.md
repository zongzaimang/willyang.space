The SYNCO TS is a dual-channel UHF wireless microphone system for interviews, filmmaking and location recording. The design brings two transmitters and one receiver into a shared visual family while keeping channel status, physical controls and connection points immediately legible.

## One system, three coordinated units

Transmitter and receiver housings share the same compact rectangular architecture, vertical surface rhythm and contrasting antenna geometry. Subtle differences in screen, ports and control placement communicate each unit's role without breaking the identity of the set.

## Portable antennas and clear interaction

The foldable antennas become both a functional component and a recognizable silhouette. Their flat profiles sit closely against the body for transport and rotate outward for operation. Screens, top-mounted sockets and side controls are separated into clear zones to support quick adjustment in the field.

## A durable technical character

The silver-and-black CMF separates the structural body from interactive parts. Fine vertical ribs add grip and reduce the visual mass of the broad faces, while softened edges make the units comfortable to carry, clip or mount during extended production work.
