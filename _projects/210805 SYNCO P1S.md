## Wireless microphone system for smartphones

The SYNCO P1S pairs a compact clip-on transmitter with a receiver that connects directly to a smartphone. A sliding charging case keeps the components together, bringing storage, charging and setup into one compact object. Slim forms and visible status lighting give the system a consistent identity while keeping the recording interface simple and easy to carry.
